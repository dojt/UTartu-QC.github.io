Teaching

LTAT.04.021 Introduction to Quantum Information ("IQI")

  • Spring term
  • IQI introduces the quantum information concepts (qubits etc) with minimal math — but fully rigorous.
  • Instructor: Theis

LTFY.03.013 Fundamentals of Quantum Computing ("FunQ")

  • Fall term
  • FunQ is a 1st year introduction based on complex numbers & matrices, quantum circuits, and Python.
  • Instructor: Palge
